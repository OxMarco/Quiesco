Why Quiesco

Built for the room where you sleep

General-purpose air monitors tell you about your home. Quiesco focuses on the bedroom, tracking the things that actually keep you up at night.

  • Five sensors in one

    CO₂, temperature, humidity, ambient light and noise, sampled together so you see the whole picture.

  • Data you can act on

    High CO₂? Open a window. Too bright? Try a sleep mask. Too loud? Check the insulation or reach for earplugs.

  • Private by design

    Syncs to your phone over Bluetooth. No Wi-Fi, no cloud: your data is always yours.

  • Goes where you sleep

    Pocket-sized and deep-sleeping between samples, it runs for weeks on a charge and travels with you.

Specs

Under the hood

CO₂, temperature & humiditySensirion SCD41
Ambient lightVishay VEML7700
NoisePDM MEMS microphone
ProcessorNordic nRF52840
ConnectivityBluetooth Low Energy
Battery500 mAh LiPo, deep sleep between samples
SamplingEvery 10 seconds to 10 minutes (default 60 seconds)
DisplayE-ink (planned)

Open source

Open hardware, open firmware

Schematics, PCB, case, firmware and app are all released under GPLv3. Quiesco runs the Arduino bootloader, so you can change the firmware and flash it straight from the Arduino IDE.
