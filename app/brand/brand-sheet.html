App identity for the sleep-environment sensor: the mark, two themes that follow the landing page's day and night rooms, and the colours that carry the device's comfort verdicts onto the phone.

Kept from the landing logo

The round Q and its curled tail from landing/logo.png, so the site, the README and the app share one letter.

Counter pushed off-centre

The ring thickens at lower left and thins at upper right, so it reads as a crescent: the device's job starts when the light goes out.

Tail ends on the lamp

The amber dot is the bedside lamp from night.png, the one warm point in a violet room. It's the only place the icon uses gold.

Square, like the unit

The icon ground stays plain and square to echo the 53 × 53 mm case. No gloss or frame, so the OS mask shapes it on both iOS and Android.

Colour

Two rooms, one palette

The landing page slides between a violet night room and a blue day room, and the app does the same. Night is the default because the app is mostly opened at bedtime: deep violet grounds keep screen glare low, and the one warm accent is the lamp. Day follows the system light setting and uses the brand blue.

Comfort verdicts

The device's three bands, in colour

The e-ink panel is 1-bit, so it marks a problem by inverting a tile. The phone has colour, and it uses these three for the same verdicts, with the thresholds from ui/ComfortEvaluation. They're reserved for verdicts: never a brand accent, never a chart series. Each always appears with an icon and a word, and the words are the nudges the device already prints.

Each pair is Day / Night. The status set and the series set below were run through a colour-vision check against the White and Dusk card grounds. Every adjacent pair separates for deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia (ΔE ≥ 8.9), and all colours clear 3:1 against their card.

Series · comparing nights

Each metric gets its own chart and axis, so metrics don't need their own hues. A chart normally draws one line in Quiesco Blue over its comfort band. These four colours, used in this order, are for overlaying up to four nights of the same metric. There's no gold series, because gold would read as warn.
