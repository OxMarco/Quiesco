Four directions

Each one pairs a logo with a warm, natural palette, and each takes its idea from a different part of the device or the landing page. The type stays as you approved it: Kiwi Maru for the name, Fredoka for readings, Noto Sans for everything else. Logos and palettes can be mixed. Verdict colours (comfortable, warn, bad) get tuned to whichever palette you pick, so they aren't shown here.
